ABOUT LENARD MONTESSORI SCHOOL

Raising confident learners with character.

Lenard Montessori School is committed to providing a supportive learning environment where children develop knowledge, practical skills, creativity and strong values. We aim to help every learner grow into a responsible and confident member of society.

Our history: Established in 2020, Lenard Montessori School continues its commitment to learning, character development and the growth of every learner.

OUR MISSION

To provide high-quality, child-centred education that nurtures curiosity, builds strong academic foundations, develops practical abilities and instils integrity, humility and a strong work ethic in every learner.

OUR VISION

To become a first-class school recognised for academic excellence, excellent character, creativity and the development of lifelong learners who contribute positively to their communities.
